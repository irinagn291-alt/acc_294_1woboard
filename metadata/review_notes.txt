There is no login or accounts, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

1Woboard is a door till for volunteers running a single-screen repertory cinema or community hall. One person keeps the floor on screen: they place a station, clock a volunteer in, cover that station, ring walk-in admissions onto tonight's till, and advance the pair that is on deck. The house stays on this device.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch shows a dark system launch screen, then the words "1Woboard" and "Opening the floor." On an iPhone or iPad, if onboarding has not been finished, three foyer pages follow. Each page has a page mark written as "1 of 3", "2 of 3", or "3 of 3". "Skip" is on every page and finishes onboarding the same way "Continue" does. Page 1 is "A door till for the hall" with "Next" and "Skip". Page 2 is "Cover, then ring" with "Next" and "Skip". Page 3 is "Advance the pair" with "Continue" and "Skip". After "Continue" or "Skip", the empty floor appears with "The floor is empty" and "Place the first station". Type a "Name" and tap "Place station". Under "Also tonight", open "Shifts" ("Clock a volunteer in or out"). Enter a "Volunteer name", tap "Add volunteer", then "Clock in". On the floor, tap "Cover" when Health is 60 or more, then tap "Ring". A "Covered" "Ring" writes a "Till" ticket for 12 in the device region's currency. "File incident" opens the incident slate; draw, then tap "Save incident". When Health is under 60, "Maintain" appears and raises Health. Under "Also tonight", open "Bracket" ("Seat the next pair"). Enter a "Bout name", add two, four, or eight names with "Person name" and "Add", then tap "Seed bout". Tap "Advance" on an "On deck" name. Under "Also tonight", open "Door" ("Read a comp or member code"). If the camera is not yet asked, tap "Continue" to show the system camera alert, or use "Type a code" and "File code". A code must contain a run of 8 to 14 digits. Under "Also tonight", open "Finance" ("Reconcile tonight's float") to see "House float" and, after a "Till", "Export tonight CSV". Under "Also tonight", open "Settings" ("Contact, replay, or reset") for "Replay onboarding", "Reset all data", and "Contact Callboard". On the iOS Simulator, the first launch already finishes onboarding and opens a filled floor; "Replay onboarding" in Settings is how those three pages appear there.

Data stays on this device. Settings says "House on this device". On-screen support is "Contact Callboard" on Settings, which opens the support page.
